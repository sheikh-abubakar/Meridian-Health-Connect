Immutable clinical history

Controller and Mongoose hooks reject finalized note/AI edits. Amendments append.

Field masking

Front-desk APIs and exports exclude diagnosis, note, AI and task outcome detail.

Account lifecycle

Hashes are hidden; staff removal deactivates accounts without deleting references.

POC boundary: demonstrated controls do not constitute regulatory certification. Production needs formal threat modelling, privacy assessment, penetration testing, retention/key policies and jurisdiction review.

08 · State integrity

Consequential transitions are explicit

Appointment

scheduled → checked_in → completed. Finalizing the linked encounter causes completion; invalid skips/repeats are rejected.

Encounter

draft → save/optional AI acceptance → finalized → append-only amendments.

Task

General tasks complete normally. Outreach tasks require agreed, declined or unreachable; agreed creates one recall.

RecallRequest

pending_scheduling → Front-desk selects valid slot → scheduled with appointmentId. Never auto-booked.
